About these figures

These are Tanushri's metrics, and no one else's. Values are filled in only from what her public profile shows at the time of observation, so none are estimated or assumed here.

Until the watcher has completed its first observation, every entry reads as pending. The state file created on that first run becomes the baseline for every comparison that follows.

How a change is identified

The first execution writes leetcode_state.json, a snapshot of Tanushri's publicly visible data. Every later observation is compared against that snapshot, and any meaningful difference triggers a notification.

No database or long in-memory history is needed. The system retains only what is required to detect change, which keeps it small enough to run continuously.

Why 250 seconds?

Observation interval

250 seconds provides a sufficiently frequent observation interval to maintain current awareness of Tanushri's progress without introducing unnecessary request volume.

A shorter interval would add requests without adding information, since public profile data changes at the pace of her submissions rather than by the second. A much longer interval would allow several solutions to accumulate unobserved between checks. At 250 seconds, roughly fourteen observations occur every hour, and each accepted solution is reported while it is still recent.

Why this level of attention is warranted

Competitive context

Speed and consistency

Tanushri solves problems quickly and does so day after day. Pace sustained over time is the hardest thing to build, and the clearest case for observing it continuously.

Increasing difficulty

Her progress is not limited to volume. The problems get harder, which makes the Easy, Medium and Hard distribution one of the most informative things to track.

Discipline and ranking

Competitive programming rewards steady habits. Her ranking movement is the measurable result of that discipline, and it deserves an accurate record.

Tanushri is sufficiently good that her progress has become something worth measuring continuously. If she is solving problems, the system should know. If her ranking changes, the system should know.

System philosophy

The purpose is not merely to collect numbers. It is to maintain an accurate understanding of Tanushri's trajectory over time.

Her progress is measurable. Her consistency is measurable. Her improvement is measurable. Her ability to solve increasingly difficult problems is measurable. Continuous measurement is therefore not optional. It is infrastructure.

Distributed observation

Deployment direction

There is no technical reason for awareness of Tanushri's progress to remain limited to a single machine. The watcher is small enough to run on personal computers, home servers, VPS instances or cloud infrastructure.

Multiple instances could observe independently and cross-check one another, and alerts could extend beyond desktop notifications to Telegram, Discord webhooks or a tray application. SQLite history would allow trend analysis across weeks rather than single observations. These are logical next steps for the project.

Scope and limits

Important

This system observes public information only.

It does not log into any account, bypass authentication, access private submissions, or attempt to circumvent LeetCode protections. It stores no credentials and collects no personal or contact information.

The recent-submission data available through the public interface is limited, so this is a lightweight activity monitor rather than a complete historical archive.
